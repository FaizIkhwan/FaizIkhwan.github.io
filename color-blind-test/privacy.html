Privacy Policy

Color Blind Test (Android and iOS app com.fi.colorblindtest) · Effective 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information the Color Blind Test app ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by Faiz Ikhwan.

The short version: there are no accounts, and you never enter your name, email or any other personal details. The app uses Google services to show ads, count how the app is used and report crashes. If you buy "Remove ads", Google Play or the Apple App Store handles the payment; we never see your payment details. Those services collect device identifiers and usage data, as described below.

1. Information we collect

WhatCollected byWhy
App usage events: app opened, test started, each plate answered (plate number, whether the answer was right, whether it was typed, skipped or timed out, and how long it took), test finished (score and result band), test abandoned or inconclusive, ad events (requested, loaded, shown, failed), and "Remove ads" purchase events (started, completed, restored, cancelled, failed). On iOS the app doesn't ask for tracking permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is never available to it or its ad partners. Google AdMob and its ad partners To show ads (one per completed test), limit fraud, and measure ad performance. Depending on your consent and region, ads may be personalised.
Purchases: if you buy "Remove ads", your store account, payment details and the transaction record. Google Play or the Apple App Store (we receive only a confirmation that the purchase was made) To process the payment and let you restore the purchase on a new device
On your device only: which plates you've already seen, so the next test shows different ones, and whether you've bought "Remove ads". The app (never sent anywhere) Plate rotation, and turning ads off after a purchase

Your test results are not linked to your name or any account. The app is for entertainment and is not a medical assessment. We don't treat your results as health data and don't use them to diagnose anything.

2. Third-party services

The app uses these Google services, which process data under their own privacy terms:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics: Firebase privacy and security
  • Google AdMob: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

Purchases are processed by the store you downloaded the app from, under its own terms:

  • Google Play: Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store: Apple Privacy Policy

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share it with anyone other than these service providers.

3. Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent the first time you open it, using Google's consent tool. Until you choose, Google Analytics runs in a restricted mode that doesn't store identifiers on your device, and ads aren't personalised. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time from Privacy settings on the app's home screen.

4. Your choices everywhere

  • Android: reset your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Google → Ads.
  • iOS: the app never asks to track you. You can turn off Apple's own personalised ads in Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes the plate history stored on your device. A "Remove ads" purchase stays with your store account; use Restore purchase on the home screen to get it back.

5. How long data is kept

User-level Google Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics (at most 14 months), then deleted. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. AdMob data is kept under Google's policies linked above. Purchase records are kept by Google Play or Apple under their own policies.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. The app doesn't know who you are, so we usually can't link data to you directly. If you contact us we'll help where we can, for example by deleting Analytics data linked to your app-instance ID if you can provide it.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for analytics and ads. For crash reporting we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

7. Children

The app is meant for a general audience aged 13 and over. It isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we'll help delete any related data.

8. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we only use reputable providers and collect as little as we need.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing.
