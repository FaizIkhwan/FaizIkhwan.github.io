EjectWater Speaker Cleaner

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

In short: EjectWater has no accounts, and it never asks for your name, email, photos, contacts, location or microphone. The app plays a 165 Hz tone through your speaker, and the tone is generated on your device. To keep the app free and working properly, we use Google services that collect limited device and usage data: AdMob for ads, Firebase Analytics for usage statistics and Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports. In the EU, UK and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent before it loads ads or turns on usage statistics, and you can change your answer at any time. If you buy “Remove ads”, Google Play or the Apple App Store handles the payment, and we never see your payment details.

This Privacy Policy explains how EjectWater Speaker Cleaner (the “App”), developed by Faiz Ikhwan (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use it on Android or iOS, and when you visit our website (see Our website).

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or enter any personal details.
  • The App does not access your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, files or precise location.
  • The cleaning tone is generated and played on your device and is never sent anywhere.
  • We never receive your card number, billing address or other payment details.

Information collected by third-party services

The App includes the following Google services. They collect information automatically, under their own privacy policies.

In-app purchases

The App offers one optional, one-time purchase, “Remove ads”. When you buy or restore it:

  • Payment is processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store, under their own terms and privacy policies. They know which account made the purchase. We do not.
  • The store tells the App that the purchase succeeded and gives it a purchase record (the product ID and a transaction reference). The App uses it only to switch ads off. The record is not sent to us or stored on any server of ours.
  • The App saves a small “ads removed” setting on your device so ads stay off when you are offline. Uninstalling the App deletes it. You can get the purchase back for free with “Restore purchases” in the App’s info sheet, or automatically when you reinstall while signed in to the same store account.
  • Firebase Analytics may automatically record that an in-app purchase happened, including the product and price, as part of its standard purchase reporting. This is not linked to your name or payment details.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. You can limit this at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, then reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • iOS: The App does not ask for permission to track you, so Google does not receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) on iOS.
  • Remove ads: after you buy “Remove ads”, the App stops showing ads and no longer starts the AdMob SDK, so AdMob stops collecting data through the App.
  • Consent choices: in the EU, UK and Switzerland you can decline or change consent for ads and usage statistics. See Your consent choices.

Your consent choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App shows a consent form from Google the first time you open it. The form explains how AdMob and Firebase Analytics would use your data, for example for personalised ads, and lets you agree or decline.

  • Until you have answered, the App does not load any ads and Firebase Analytics stays switched off.
  • Your answer is passed to AdMob and Firebase Analytics, and they only collect and use data in the ways you agreed to. If you decline personalised ads, you may still see ads that are not based on your data.
  • If the form cannot load, for example because you are offline, the App uses the answer you gave before. If you have never answered, ads and analytics stay off.
  • You can change your answer at any time: open the App’s info sheet (the ⓘ button, or “How it works” on tablets) and tap Privacy choices. This option only appears where the consent form is required.
  • Crash reports from Firebase Crashlytics are not part of this choice. They are sent so we can find and fix crashes, and they contain the technical details listed above, nothing that identifies you by name.

Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: needed to load ads, to send analytics and crash reports, and to reach the app store for purchases.
  • Billing (Android): lets the App start a purchase through Google Play. It gives the App no access to your payment methods.
  • Keep screen awake: stops the screen from sleeping during a cleaning cycle.
  • Media volume: while the tone plays, the App temporarily sets your media volume to maximum. Your previous volume is restored when the cycle ends.

Data retention and security

We do not store any data on our own servers. Purchase records are kept by Google Play or Apple, and the “ads removed” setting stays on your device until you uninstall the App. Data collected by the services above is kept and protected by Google under its own retention policies. For example, Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Our website

Our website, eject-water-speaker.web.app, has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it does not set cookies. It is hosted on Google’s Firebase Hosting, so Google receives your IP address and basic browser details when you visit, which it needs to deliver the page. Its fonts are served from the same site, so no other service is contacted. The test tone on the website is generated in your browser, and nothing about it is recorded or sent anywhere.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example in the EU, UK or California, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. We hold no personal data that is linked to you, so most requests are best sent straight to Google using the links above. You can also contact us and we will help where we can. Where the App asked for your consent, you can withdraw it at any time with Privacy choices. Uninstalling the App stops all further data collection.

Changes to this policy
