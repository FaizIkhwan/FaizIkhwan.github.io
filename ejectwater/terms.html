What the App does

When you start a cleaning cycle, the App sets your media volume to maximum and plays a 165 Hz tone for about 30 seconds. The vibration can help push water out of the speaker grille. Your previous volume is restored when the cycle ends. The cycle stops automatically if you connect headphones, receive a call or leave the App.

Safe use

  • Protect your hearing. The tone plays at full volume. Keep the phone away from your ears and do not hold the speaker against your head.
  • Do not use the App near people or animals who may be sensitive to loud sounds.
  • Turn the phone off and dry it before using the App if the device has been submerged or is behaving abnormally.
  • Do not run the App while the device is charging if it is still wet.
