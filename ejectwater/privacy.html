EjectWater Speaker Cleaner

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

In short: EjectWater has no accounts, and it never asks for your name, email, photos, contacts, location or microphone. The app plays a 165 Hz tone through your speaker, and the tone is generated on your device. To keep the app free and working properly, we use Google services that collect limited device and usage data: AdMob for ads, Firebase Analytics for usage statistics and Firebase Crashlytics for crash reports.

This Privacy Policy explains how EjectWater Speaker Cleaner (the “App”), developed by Faiz Ikhwan (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use it on Android or iOS.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account or enter any personal details.
  • The App does not access your microphone, camera, photos, contacts, files or precise location.
  • The cleaning tone is generated and played on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Information collected by third-party services

The App includes the following Google services. They collect information automatically, under their own privacy policies.

ServicePurposeData collected
Google AdMob Shows the banner ad that keeps the App free Device advertising identifier (where permitted), IP address, device model, operating system, language, and ad interaction data such as views and taps
Firebase Analytics Helps us understand how the App is used so we can improve it App instance ID, device model, OS version, country/region (derived from IP), app version, and in-app events such as when a cleaning cycle starts, finishes or is stopped early, and how long it ran
Firebase Crashlytics Lets us find and fix crashes Crash traces, device model, OS version, app version, and device state at the time of the crash (such as free memory and orientation)

The analytics events we log contain no personal information. They only record what the App did, for example “cleaning completed after 30 seconds”.

Advertising

Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and Google may use your device's advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you. You can limit this at any time:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads, then reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • iOS: The App does not ask for permission to track you, so Google does not receive your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) on iOS.

Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: needed to load ads and to send analytics and crash reports.
  • Keep screen awake: stops the screen from sleeping during a cleaning cycle.
  • Media volume: while the tone plays, the App temporarily sets your media volume to maximum. Your previous volume is restored when the cycle ends.

Data retention and security

We do not store any data on our own servers. Data collected by the services above is kept and protected by Google under its own retention policies. For example, Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you think a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, for example in the EU, UK or California, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to its processing. We hold no personal data that is linked to you, so most requests are best sent straight to Google using the links above. You can also contact us and we will help where we can. Uninstalling the App stops all further data collection.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.
