Privacy Policy

Quick Trivia for Android phone, tablet and Android TV

Effective date: 3 October 2026

  • No account and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your game progress and settings stay on your device.
  • The app uses Google services for ads (phone and tablet only), in-app purchases, crash reports and usage statistics. These services collect some device data as described below.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Quick Trivia ("the app", "we", "us") is an offline trivia game published on Google Play by Faiz Ikhwan. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. If you have a question, contact us at faizikhwantop@gmail.com.

2. Information stored only on your device

The following is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded to us:

  • Game progress: which question sets you have played and your best scores.
  • Settings, such as whether sound is on.
  • Your ad consent choice and whether usage statistics are enabled.
  • A record of purchases you have made (for example, removing ads or unlocking all question sets), so they keep working when you are offline.

Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes this information. All trivia questions are bundled with the app, so playing does not require an internet connection.

3. Information collected by third-party services

The app only connects to the internet for the Google services listed here. Each is operated by Google and handles data under Google's Privacy Policy.

Google AdMob (advertising)

On phones and tablets, the app may show a full-screen ad after a round and offers an optional rewarded ad. No ads are shown on Android TV, and none are shown once you buy the remove-ads option. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect:

  • Your device's advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your device's Google settings);
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system version, language and ad interactions.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before requesting ads, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings. Where personalised ads are not permitted, non-personalised ads may still be shown. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The app receives only a confirmation of what was purchased so it can unlock the feature.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

If the app crashes or hits a serious error, a report is sent so we can fix the problem. It contains technical information such as the device model, operating system version, app version, the state of the app at the time of the crash and a random installation identifier. It does not contain your name or contact details.

Google Analytics for Firebase (usage statistics)

We collect anonymous gameplay statistics to understand how the app is used and to tune question difficulty. These include events such as a round being completed (score and question set), a question being answered (correct or not), a purchase being completed and a rewarded ad being watched. Firebase also records a random app-instance identifier and general device information (model, OS version, language, approximate country).

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, analytics follows the consent choice you make in the consent form.

5. Device permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads, process purchases and send crash and usage reports, and to check whether you are online.
  • Billing: to make in-app purchases through Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: used by AdMob as described above.

The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location.

6. Data retention

Information on your device is kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Crash reports and analytics data are kept by Firebase for a limited time according to Google's retention settings and then deleted. Advertising data is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Change your ad consent in Settings → Ad privacy settings.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Google settings.
  • Delete all local data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we do not collect your name or contact details, we usually cannot link data held by Google to you; if you contact us we will help where we can. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Children

The app is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Security

Data sent to Google services is encrypted in transit. Information stored on your device is protected by Android's app sandbox.

10. International transfers

Google may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States, under its own safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Play Store listing.
